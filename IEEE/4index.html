About ICIETSD 2026

Join leading researchers, academicians, and industry experts to share innovations and ideas in sustainable and emerging technologies

Shaping the Future of Sustainable Technology

The 2026 International Conference on Innovations in Emerging Technologies for Sustainable Development (ICIETSD) scheduled on April 9–10, 2026 aims to bring together global thought leaders, researchers, and practitioners to share groundbreaking innovations and ideas at the intersection of sustainability and emerging technologies.

The conference fosters cross-disciplinary collaboration in critical fields like computing, communication, infrastructure, energy, environment, and security to drive a more sustainable and technologically advanced future.

Conference Objectives

Our mission is to promote research and innovation in cutting-edge technologies that address global sustainability challenges

Encourage Innovation

Promote breakthrough innovations in AI, Blockchain, IoT, 5G, and quantum computing to solve complex sustainability challenges across industries.

Advance Sustainability

Support development and implementation of sustainable solutions in infrastructure, energy, manufacturing, and environmental management.

Enhance Security

Strengthen cybersecurity frameworks and develop resilient digital systems for sustainable smart cities and critical infrastructure.

Foster Collaboration

Create multidisciplinary partnerships between academia, industry, government, and research institutions to accelerate sustainable technology adoption.
