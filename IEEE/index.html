About the Conference

ICIETSD 2024 aims to bring together leading academic scientists, researchers and research scholars to exchange and share their experiences and research results on all aspects of Innovative Engineering Technologies for Sustainable Development.

Scope and Topics

The conference provides an interdisciplinary platform for researchers, practitioners and educators to present and discuss the most recent innovations, trends, and concerns as well as practical challenges encountered and solutions adopted in the fields of Engineering Technologies for Sustainable Development.

Topics of interest for submission include, but are not limited to:

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Internet of Things (IoT) and Smart Systems
  • Renewable Energy Technologies
  • Sustainable Manufacturing
  • Green Building Technologies
  • Environmental Engineering

Conference Tracks

The conference covers a wide range of topics in engineering and technology

AI & Machine Learning

Artificial intelligence, deep learning, neural networks, intelligent systems, and applications in sustainable development.

IoT & Smart Systems

Internet of Things, wireless sensor networks, smart cities, industrial IoT, and connected devices for sustainability.

Data Science & Analytics

Big data analytics, data mining, predictive modeling, and data-driven decision making for sustainable solutions.

Cloud & Edge Computing

Cloud infrastructure, edge computing, distributed systems, and computing architectures for sustainable technologies.

Renewable Energy

Solar, wind, hydro, and other renewable energy technologies, energy storage, smart grids, and sustainable power systems.

Sustainable Manufacturing

Green manufacturing, circular economy, waste reduction, sustainable materials, and eco-friendly production processes.
