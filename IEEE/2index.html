About the Conference

The 2026 International Conference on Innovations in Emerging Technologies for Sustainable Development (ICIETSD) scheduled on April 9–10, 2026 aims to bring together researchers, academicians, and industry experts to share innovations and ideas in sustainable and emerging technologies.

Conference Overview

The event fosters cross-disciplinary collaboration in fields like computing, communication, infrastructure, energy, environment, and security to drive a more sustainable future.

Our Goals

Encourage Innovation

Promote research in cutting-edge areas like AI, Blockchain, IoT, and 5G

Support Sustainability

Foster eco-friendly and low-carbon technology development

Advance Security

Improve cybersecurity, digitalisation, and smart systems

Enable Collaboration

Connect academia, industry, and research communities

Conference Focus Areas

The conference focuses on sustainable solutions in infrastructure, energy, and manufacturing while addressing global challenges across sectors.

Renewable Energy Technologies

Innovations in solar, wind, and sustainable power systems

AI & Smart Systems

Artificial intelligence applications for sustainable development

IoT & 5G Networks

Connected technologies for smart cities and infrastructure

Sustainable Manufacturing

Green production processes and circular economy
